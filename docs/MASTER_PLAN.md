# Master plan

YEMENI HERITAGE — COPPER & COFFEE EDITION is a cinematic digital exhibition and portfolio celebrating Yemeni heritage through art direction, editorial storytelling and interactive frontend craft. The owner directive authorizes Phase 01 and establishes the palette and experience direction. Material changes follow CHANGE_CONTROL.md.

## Experience

The opening establishes an editorial voice against a coffee-and-copper atmosphere. The Yemeni Ibex receives its own sculptural presentation. Bab Al-Yemen shifts to bronze and warm sandstone. Dar Al-Hajar moves into muted olive and natural stone. Two independent coffee objects close the exhibition and lead to a client contact action.

## Phase 01

Lay down permanent governance, a strict Next.js App Router project, bilingual routes, responsive navigation, scene-specific tokens, a cinematic hero, four subsequent scene containers, independent coffee object definitions and restrained, accessible motion. Prepare lighting, asset and camera contracts. Validate build, types, lint, and browser behavior where available.

## Later phases

Require owner approval before starting later phases. Use approved references to commission or source the Ibex, Bab Al-Yemen and Dar Al-Hajar assets. Introduce selectively loaded WebGL, camera choreography and independent coffee motion after reference, performance and accessibility checks. Preserve Phase 01 fallback compositions for constrained devices.

Keep completed designs and decisions unless evidence warrants change. Never substitute fabricated historical details for a reference.
